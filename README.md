# 📊 AI & Data Science Job Market Analysis

## 🚀 Project Overview
This project analyzes the global AI & Data Science job market using real-world data from Kaggle. The goal is to identify trends in job roles, hiring locations, company sizes, and industry demand.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze in-demand job roles in AI & Data Science
- Identify top hiring countries
- Understand company hiring patterns
- Explore remote vs onsite job trends
- Discover industry-wise demand

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Python 🐍
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab

---

## 📂 Dataset
- Source: Kaggle  
- Dataset: AI Job Market Trends 2026  

---

## 📊 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

### 🔹 Top Job Roles
- Identified most in-demand roles like AI Engineer and Data Analyst

### 🔹 Company Size Distribution
- Large companies and startups dominate hiring

### 🔹 Country-wise Analysis
- Countries like Germany and Singapore lead in job opportunities

### 🔹 Remote vs Onsite Jobs
- Increasing trend of remote and hybrid work

### 🔹 Industry Analysis
- Technology and E-commerce industries are leading hiring sectors

---

## 🧠 Key Insights
- AI & Data Science roles are rapidly growing worldwide  
- Remote work is becoming more common  
- Tech industry dominates hiring demand  
- Global opportunities are expanding in developed countries
